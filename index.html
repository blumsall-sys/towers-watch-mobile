TOWER DEFENSE

Towers Watch

Move between the north, east, south and west tower standards. Each breach damages the shared tower. In the final wave, a new front opens every nine seconds. Knights attack the archer in melee range and strike the tower if they reach the standard. Keep both health bars above zero.
